# Music credits

## Quiet piano recordings

All four recordings are in the **public domain**. They came from Wikimedia Commons as MP3 versions of the original files. They were picked because they measured the calmest of the candidates: low background noise, small volume swings and no loud peaks.

| File | Piece | Performer | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| satie-gymnopedie-1.mp3 | Erik Satie — Gymnopédie No. 1 | Robin Alciatore | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Erik_Satie_-_gymnopedies_-_la_1_ere._lent_et_douloureux.ogg |
| satie-gnossienne-1.mp3 | Erik Satie — Gnossienne No. 1 | La Pianista | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Satie_-_Gnossienne_1.ogg |
| satie-gnossienne-3.mp3 | Erik Satie — Gnossienne No. 3 | La Pianista | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gnossienne_3_(Satie).ogg |
| schumann-traumerei.mp3 | Robert Schumann — Träumerei (Kinderszenen No. 7) | Musopen | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Robert_Schumann_-_scenes_from_childhood,_op._15_-_vii._dreaming.ogg |

## Calm and soft guitar

The "Calm (sleep-style)" and "Soft guitar" music is generated live in the browser by `src/lib/calm.js`, so it has no audio files and no license terms.
