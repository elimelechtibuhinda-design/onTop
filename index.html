Dialed In — the free study app for students

Dialed In helps you focus, plan and actually study. It runs in your browser and installs on your phone or laptop like an app. Free, with no account and no email.

Focus

  • Pomodoro (25/5, 50/10 or your own lengths) or a free study timer
  • Pause when you get distracted. Dialed In tracks your distracted time separately from your study time
  • A pop-out mini timer that floats beside your other tabs and apps
  • Calm, sleep-style study music, soft guitar and quiet piano, plus focus sounds

Plan

  • Add your courses and exam dates and get a study plan for each exam
  • SMART goals, daily tasks and a weekly planner
  • Flashcards with pictures and math formulas, plus spaced review reminders

Stay motivated

  • Daily and weekly stats: focused vs distracted time, streaks and your best focus hours
  • XP, levels, ranks, badges and a garden that grows as you study
  • Short break games that lock when your break is over

Private by design

No sign-up. Your study data stays on your device, works offline, and can sync between your devices with end-to-end encryption.
